Tochi privacy policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Tochi is a small pixel mascot who lives in your widgets and is as tired or as bright as you are. This page says plainly what the app does with your information. Short version: your health data stays on your phone.

What Tochi reads

With your permission, Tochi reads two kinds of data from Apple Health: sleep and steps. It only reads. It never writes anything to Health.

If you do not track your sleep, Tochi can estimate your night from step counts, on your device, and the answer you can give to his optional morning question stays on your device too and is never backed up or sent anywhere.

If you say no, Tochi still works. He just goes by the time of day.

Tochi uses your phone's time zone, on your phone, to estimate when it is light outside, so the window can follow the sun. For this, no location is requested and nothing is sent.

Where it goes

Your health data goes nowhere. Your sleep and step data is processed on your device and does not leave it. Tochi has no server of its own.

One thing does leave your phone: to draw the weather in Tochi's window, the app sends the coordinates of the city your phone's time zone is named after to Apple's weather service. No location is requested of your phone and no health data is sent. As with any request, Apple's service also sees your phone's network address.

To draw the widget, the app saves a small file on your device with today's values. The widget reads that file. The file is excluded from device backups. The large widget hides the step count and sleep words while your phone is locked.

What Tochi does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No third-party code. Apple's weather service is the only outside service the app contacts.
  • No tracking.

Purchases

Tochi+ is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details. It only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made.

Stopping access and deleting data

You can turn off Tochi's access to sleep and steps at any time in the Health app (Sharing, then Apps, then Tochi) or in the Settings app. Deleting Tochi from your phone deletes everything the app has stored.

Children

Tochi is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
