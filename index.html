Common questions

How do I put Tochi on my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Tochi. He comes in small, medium and large.

And on the Lock Screen?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, and add a Tochi widget.

Tochi is not following my sleep or steps.

Tochi reads sleep and steps from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap Sharing, then Apps, then Tochi, and turn on Sleep and Steps. If you would rather not share them, Tochi still works. He goes by the time of day.

The widget looks out of date.

Open Tochi once and the widgets catch up. For them to keep up on their own, turn on Background App Refresh for Tochi in the Settings app.

I do not track my sleep.

Tochi guesses your night from your steps, and in the morning he can ask how you slept. One tap answers him. You can turn the question off in Tochi's settings.

The weather in his window is not my weather.

Tochi never asks for your location. He shows the weather for the city your phone's time zone is named after, which may be some way from you.

I bought Tochi+ and the colours are locked.

In Tochi, open settings and tap restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
